# 09 TeX Formalization

## Purpose

Produce the formal TeX manuscript without changing its science.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), actual author approval of the source
transition, the active manuscript, references, assets and applicable venue template.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the approved transition and local source authority; do not create two
   independently maintained manuscripts.
2. Convert the actual prose, equations, citations, figures and tables. Preserve
   scientific content and disclose any necessary representational change.
3. Compile, repair direct errors and inspect the resulting PDF. Record the source
   transition in the existing authority, not a freeze receipt or new log.

## Output

Compilable TeX, the resulting PDF, included references/assets and any unresolved
content issue. Do not invent author approval or rewrite the contribution.

## Validation

Compile the changed source and inspect affected pages and cross-references.
Repeat only after an actual correction. Compilation is not scientific validation.
