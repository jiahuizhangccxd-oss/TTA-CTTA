# Scientific Writing

## Purpose

Support readable prose from stable scientific content, without creating evidence
or changing claim strength.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), the supplied paragraph content,
intellectual purpose, domain terminology and adjacent argument.

## Workflow

1. Identify the paragraph's point and the claims, examples, equations, citations,
   numbers and boundaries that must remain.
2. State the point clearly and order reasoning so adjacent paragraphs connect.
   Consult only the relevant Paper construction subsection of the research loop.
3. Express the actual scientific objects rather than developer operations.
   Preserve meaningful agent/tool/pipeline terms; do not manufacture a formulation
   by replacing a wrapper or interface with a scientific noun.
4. Remove repetition without deleting unique technical content or uncertainty.

## Output

Manuscript-ready prose retaining supplied content and evidence strength. Do not
add headings, disclaimers or framework language merely to sound academic.

## Validation

Compare the revised paragraph with its source once. Any missing evidence remains
unresolved; do not silently supply a mechanism, causal interpretation or result.
