# 02 Research Question

## Purpose

Retain, revise, merge, investigate or reject one research direction.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), the candidate's problem boundary,
observed evidence, closest verified alternative and existing author decisions.

## Workflow

1. State the question and the failure or contradiction motivating it.
2. Compare mechanisms and assumptions with the closest original work, not names,
   module counts or publication counts.
3. Identify the strongest competing explanation, divergent prediction and
   rejection condition.
4. Define the smallest fair comparison and the claims actually needed. Label
   each by theory, experiment, literature or hypothesis support.
5. Recommend a direction and next action. The author retains final approval.

## Output

The bounded question or rejection decision, meaningful prior-art difference,
minimal claim tree, decisive test and next action. No idea-quality scores or
promotion ceremony.

## Validation

Check that the distinction is supported by the original sources, the prediction
can distinguish the alternatives and no hypothesis is presented as a finding.
