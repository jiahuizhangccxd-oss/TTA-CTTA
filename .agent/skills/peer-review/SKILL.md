# Peer Review

## Purpose

Identify the scientific defects that materially affect a manuscript's contribution
and credibility. Review-only mode does not edit it.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), the requested manuscript scope,
actual claims and evidence, local venue/contribution constraints and known findings.

## Workflow

1. State the question, proposed contribution, strongest evidence and largest gap.
2. Make one focused review across only relevant dimensions: closest prior,
   theory/counterexamples, method, experiment/statistics, generalization,
   reproducibility and scientific expression.
   For requested Nature-style review, use the verified `nature-reviewer` source
   in [task-specific selection](../../references/task_specific_skill_selection.md)
   as this task's support. Adopt its claim-dependent review axes and traceable
   concern format within this single pass. Keep venue criteria specific to the
   actual target and article type; do not import its default three-report,
   synthesis and forensic-audit package. A bounded reference use is not execution
   of that whole upstream workflow.
3. Verify claims against source/results and separate independent defects from
   repeated formulations of the same issue. Check implementation-as-contribution
   and repository-language leakage without a word blacklist.
4. Prioritize conclusion-invalidating and contribution-weakening findings. Give
   exact locations, evidence, failure mechanism and the minimum useful correction.
   Distinguish a demonstrated error, an unsupported inference and inaccessible
   evidence; define the observation or change that would resolve each major issue.
5. For an authorized revision, correct the actual paper and do one final QA.
   Do not launch reviewer personas or repeat loops to manufacture confidence.

## Output

A concise prioritized review, surviving contribution, strongest counterexample
and ordered minimal revision. Give a decision recommendation only when requested;
persist a review file only when requested. No finding quota or matrix of passes.

## Validation

Check every material finding against inspected evidence. Do not mistake missing
internal logs for scientific flaws or unseen content for absence. Report access
limitations and do not claim independent review while silently editing the paper.
