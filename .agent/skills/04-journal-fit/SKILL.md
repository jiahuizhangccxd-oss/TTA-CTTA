# 04 Journal Fit

## Purpose

Inform a concrete venue or submission strategy decision.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), actual paper scope and evidence,
author-selected target and current official requirements.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, contribution, article type and actual maturity.
2. Verify official scope, file requirements and relevant recent papers.
3. Explain fit and mismatch using comparable contributions, not prestige scores.
4. Recommend the minimum manuscript changes or a target/backup/defer decision.
   Do not silently change an author-selected venue.

## Output

A source-grounded venue recommendation and necessary substantive changes.

## Validation

Check current official policies and cited examples; separate policy from editorial
inference. Formatting does not repair a contribution or scope mismatch.
