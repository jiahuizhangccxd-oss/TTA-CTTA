# 03 Literature Deep Research

## Purpose

Resolve a source-dependent novelty or positioning question.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), the exact novelty assertion, local
scientific scope and original literature sufficient to assess overlap.

## Workflow

1. Identify the missing comparison or source evidence.
2. Search the closest competitors, equivalent terminology, neighboring fields,
   relevant theory, earlier versions and reports of the same failure.
3. Read the original support for each used claim. A snippet, absent abstract
   phrase, prestige or citation count is not evidence of a mechanism or gap.
4. Compare questions, assumptions, results, limitations, exact overlap and the
   remaining distinction. Include contrary and non-comparable evidence.
   For a requested comparison matrix, define discriminating dimensions from the
   scientific question and nearest work. Trace every cell to an original page,
   section, table or figure; distinguish addressed, partially addressed, explicitly
   absent and not established. Unreported is not absent, and Ours need not win
   every dimension. Compare methods with methods, reviews with reviews.
5. Revise the requested section or novelty decision; stop when the bounded
   question is resolved. Do not impose systematic-review artifacts on ordinary
   related work; follow them when the local review protocol requires them.

## Output

A cited novelty decision, comparison or revised section; distinguish source
findings from the paper's synthesis and state unresolved access gaps.
For a requested paper count, report unique works, identity-verified full texts and
deep-read works separately; preprint and published versions are one work. Use an
available `project-paper-curator` for acquisition when requested; do not assume it
is installed here or count an abstract/HTML page as a downloaded paper.

## Validation

Recheck the load-bearing original passages and revised claims. A distinction must
be technically real and consequential, not a naming difference. Do not invent
absence of prior work from incomplete search.
