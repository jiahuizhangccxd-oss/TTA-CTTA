# Anti-Defensive Writing

## Purpose

Support explicit anti-defensive revision under 10-language-polish. Remove empty
self-protection, not necessary qualifications or uncertainty.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), original and adjacent text, the
supported claim, assumptions, numbers, citations and limitations to preserve.

## Workflow

1. Identify the function of each suspected phrase: empty disclaimer, necessary
   scope, evidence uncertainty, real limitation, useful contrast or repetition.
2. Delete only empty/redundant framing. State actual scope positively; reduce
   stacked hedges without strengthening the evidence. Retain contrasts carrying
   scientific meaning.
3. Keep limitations where readers need them. Do not move them out of the path to
   interpretation or invent facts to make a negative sentence sound positive.
4. Treat 'This does not mean', 'It is worth noting', '本文并不试图' and
   '需要说明的是' as contextual cues, not a blacklist. May, suggests and confidence
   intervals are not decorative obstacles to remove.

## Output

Revised text directly; changed pairs and brief reasons only when requested.
Unsupported claims require claim correction, not cosmetic confidence. Actual
reviewer responses route to 13-reviewer-response.

## Validation

Compare scientific meaning once: evidence verbs, uncertainty, numbers, citations,
assumptions, confounders, null/negative results and scope must survive. No hedge
scores, prose scanners or reviewer simulation. Adapted from the recorded MIT
upstream; preserve LICENSE.txt and repository NOTICE.md.
