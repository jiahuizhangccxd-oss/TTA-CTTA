# Scientific Critical Thinking

## Purpose

Resolve a bounded claim or design uncertainty through proportionate criticism.

## Required Evidence

[Paper Skill Core](../../PAPER_SKILL_CORE.md), the exact decision, supporting
observations, assumptions and local scientific scope.

## Workflow

1. Separate observation, inference, mechanism interpretation and generalization.
2. Identify the strongest competing explanation.
3. Inspect only threats that can change the conclusion: confounding, leakage,
   dependence, mismatch among question/estimand/metric, implementation error,
   selective reporting or unsupported causal/general claims.
4. Name the smallest discriminating observation, intervention or source check.
5. Retain, narrow, revise, reject or leave unresolved; apply the correction only
   when authorized. Do not expand a bounded critique into a full-paper review.

## Output

The justified conclusion, located material concerns, decisive correction and
remaining boundary. Formal bias/ethics frameworks apply only when required.

## Validation

Check each concern against actual evidence and distinguish missing support from
contradiction. Do not manufacture findings, score claim quality or start repeated
reviewer rounds.
